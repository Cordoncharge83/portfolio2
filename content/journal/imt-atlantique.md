## The programme

A three-year engineering degree at IMT Atlantique, one of France's grandes écoles, specialising in software engineering. The curriculum covered software architecture, distributed systems and microservices, DevOps practices, and advanced algorithms, alongside the broader engineering foundations the school requires of all its students.

## How I used it

The degree is structured around long internships and project work rather than lectures alone. I spent a semester at La Banque Postale building a retrieval-augmented assistant, then six months at Sopra Steria maintaining a large healthcare system, and in between worked on academic projects including a VR simulation in Unity and a game-development workshop. I also spent a year leading WAKE, the student committee behind the school's largest event.

## What it gave me

A grounding in how software is designed, tested and operated at scale, and enough breadth to move between backend systems, AI applications and interactive work without starting from scratch each time.
