## Context

WAKE is the student association responsible for IMT Atlantique's largest annual event, which draws up to 500 participants. Organising it takes most of a year and involves partnerships, communication, budgeting and logistics, all handled by students alongside their studies.

## Role

I led the 20-member organising committee and was responsible for public relations. That meant finding and managing partners, coordinating communication, and keeping the different workstreams aligned as the event approached. Much of the work was making decisions with incomplete information and ensuring that twenty people with different priorities delivered a single coherent event.

## What I learned

How to coordinate a team, negotiate with external stakeholders, and deliver on a fixed date. It's the least technical entry here, but it's the one I rely on most when a project has more than one person involved.
