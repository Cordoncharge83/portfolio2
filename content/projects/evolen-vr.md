A virtual reality game commissioned by EVOLEN and built by a multidisciplinary team of five to six IMT Atlantique students to introduce careers in the energy transition. I worked on gameplay programming: player movement, object interactions, level progression, and NPC dialogue with options unlocked by player actions and completed objectives. In the solar scene, the player walks around a photovoltaic site where characters describe their jobs on dialogue panels, and collecting tokens unlocks further questions. The biogas scene reuses the same setup with other characters, plus panels explaining the anaerobic digester and the gas purifier.
